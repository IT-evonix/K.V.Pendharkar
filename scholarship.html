It is the responsibility of the students/guardians to submit the scholarship/freeship application forms in time along with necessary documents as per the notifications issued by the college office.

02

It is the responsibility of the students to check notice board from time to time.

03

The college will not be responsible if the scholarship or freeship is not granted or renewed, due to non-submission or late submission of application form.

04

Scholarship and freeship is awarded for one academic year only.

05

It is necessary to submit fresh application to appropriate authorities for renewal of scholarship and freeships.

06

Application for Freeships/Scholarships etc. with the necessary certificates must reach to the Principal within the notified due date.

Photocopies of the documents must be attested by the competent authorities.

09

A receipt to the effect that application has been received by the office should be insisted upon, and the receipt should be preserved and produced as and when required especially while making the enquiry.

10

In respect of certain fee-concession/ scholarships, students will have to pay full prescribed fees at the time of admission.

11

Incomplete applications for scholarships, and freeships will not be accepted.

12

Students wish to apply for scholarship can contact college office for the details regarding Scholarships and Freeships.

13

Students are advised to be in the habit of reading the notices displayed on the Notice Boards regularly.

About Us

It was a dream and passion of Dr. Prashanth Rao and Dr. Upasni who brought together a few more like-minded people. Thus, fourteen elite citizens came together to establish Dombivli Shikshan Prasarak Mandal in 1972. Mandal’s first office was started at Shri. Desai’s residence at Narayan Krupa in Dombivli East.
